What was taken: the principles, summarised in our own words and adapted to IdeaForm. These
include one consistent design system, equal content edges, hierarchy without extra boxes, plain
naming copy, a small set of motion tokens (short ease-out hover and press feedback, one entrance,
transform and opacity only, no scroll reveals or tilt), and full reduced-motion support.

What was left out: its asset kit, dealer and checker scripts, Stop hook, photography and

a product app, not a company website, so those don't apply here. No text or code was copied
verbatim.
